Who We Are

A centralised platform for NGOs, volunteers & donors

Umang Foundation was built to close the gap between people who want to help and the causes that need it. Organisations publish campaigns and events; volunteers register in a click; donors give with full visibility into where the money goes.

From beneficiary onboarding to real-time donation tracking, every piece of the workflow lives in one dashboard — no more scattered spreadsheets or missed follow-ups.
